01 · PROFILE

About Me

DATA ANALYST

I'm a data analyst who usually starts with something I've noticed in real life rather than a clean dataset. Watching how a supermarket chain like Jendol runs its tills, branches and loyalty scheme became a 2-million-row simulated retail system, then a SQL analysis of it, then a Power BI dashboard on top.

When the right data doesn't exist, I design it: the customers, the business rules, the seasonal events and the edge cases. When it does exist, I question it. My crypto scanner is the clearest example. The first backtest looked great, and it was wrong, so I rebuilt the test until the result could be trusted.

I work in SQL, Excel, Python and Power BI. The part I care about most is the last step: turning an analysis into something a manager can actually act on.
