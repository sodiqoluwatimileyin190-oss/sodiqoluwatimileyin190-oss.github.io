About Me

I’m a Data Analyst focused on transforming real-world business observations into structured data systems, analytical insights, and decision-driven solutions. My work combines business understanding with SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, and data visualization to investigate how customers behave, where businesses lose revenue, and where operational improvements can create measurable impact.

Beyond analyzing existing datasets, I am particularly interested in understanding how data is created. I design business rules, customer behaviours, distributions, events, and transactional logic to build realistic simulated data environments when suitable real-world data is unavailable.

My portfolio demonstrates the complete analytical journey — from observing a business problem and designing the underlying data logic, to generating and engineering large-scale datasets, querying them with SQL, and communicating insights through interactive dashboards and visual storytelling.
