01 / THE PATTERN

The signal comes before the headline.

Two traders posted the same idea within days of each other: buy coins that pumped hard, bled back down and have gone completely quiet. I could see it on individual charts. The question was whether a script could find it across the whole market, early.

  • A beaten-down coin, far below its old high
  • A long, tight base: weeks or months of sideways price
  • Early signs of waking up before the big candle
Strategic Focus

Stop reacting to coins that already moved, and start watching the ones that are quietly getting ready to.

02 / VERSION 1

It ran, and the picks looked right.

The first scanner pulled daily and 4-hour candles for every liquid coin on Bybit, scored each one, and ranked them. The problems started before the first scan finished.

  • My network couldn't resolve Bybit's API address; the fix was an automatic fallback to their backup domain
  • 218 coins scored and saved to CSV, with the top picks sent straight to Telegram
  • The top-ranked coins matched what I saw when I checked their charts by hand
Strategic Focus

"Looks right on the chart" is a feeling, not evidence. Before trusting it, test it.

The fair test: every coin compared with the median coin on the same date.
03 / TESTING IT HONESTLY

The first backtest was wrong about itself.

My first backtest said low scores beat high scores. Then I noticed 81% of all coins had pumped that month, and my test only included coins that were liquid today. The test was biased toward winners. So I rebuilt it.

  • 16 weekly test dates instead of one, giving 961 samples across 132 coins
  • Coins picked by their trading volume at that date, removing survivorship bias
  • Returns measured against the median coin, so a market-wide rally can't fake a result
  • Raw data saved before the report step, after a 48-minute run crashed at the very end

04 / WHAT THE DATA SAID

Most of my "smart" signals hurt.

Overall, the score had almost no predictive power. But the factor breakdown showed exactly where the edge was, and where it wasn't.

  • Momentum signals like volume, OBV and strength vs Bitcoin tend to fire after a move has already started
  • The filters (beaten down, hasn't moved yet, liquid) carried the strongest edge in the whole test
  • Big pumps were mostly not predictable from price data alone, which pointed toward news
Strategic Focus

Keep what the evidence supports, demote what it doesn't, and add the missing ingredient: catalysts.

  • Beaten down + hasn't moved yet +7.1%
  • Long, tight price base +2.1%
  • Volatility squeeze +1.0%
  • Trend flip −0.9%
  • Volume waking up −1.3%
  • Buy-volume dominance −2.2%
  • OBV accumulation −3.0%
  • Strength vs Bitcoin −3.2%

05 / VERSION 3

Rebuilt around the evidence.

The coil became the core score. Momentum moved to a separate trigger that only decides timing. And because I didn't want to pay for anything before it proves itself, every new data source is free.

  • Public news feeds scored by keyword for catalysts, upcoming events and red flags
  • A proximity rule stops false flags: "hacker swaps ETH" no longer blacklists Ethereum
  • Four clear tiers: Trigger, Heating, Watch, and Red Flag for coins to avoid
  • Runs every 4 hours through Windows Task Scheduler, with a log of every run
Strategic Focus

A scanner is a watchlist, not a buy button. The decision still needs a chart, the news and a stop-loss.

ARCHITECTURE

Two pipelines, one shortlist.

Market data and news are processed separately, then combined into a single ranked report. Every alert is logged and checked again 3, 7 and 14 days later, so the scanner keeps grading itself.

Market Data
Bybit Public API Daily and 4-hour candles, funding rates and open interest
Filters + Coil Score
Tiering Coil, catalyst, hot sector, squeeze fuel and trigger combined
Every 4 Hours
Telegram Report Top 10 coins, upcoming events, listings and red flags
News & Events
News Feeds + Exchange Announcements Public RSS feeds, Bybit and Binance listing notices
Keyword Scoring
Catalysts, Events, Red Flags Matched to coins by ticker and name, with a 30-day news memory
Red-flagged coins are removed before ranking, so a hacked or delisted coin never reaches the shortlist.
TOOLS & TECHNIQUES

From raw candles to a tested shortlist.

Data Collection Python, ccxt, REST APIs, rate limiting, automatic domain fallback.
Feature Engineering pandas, NumPy, Bollinger width, SuperTrend, MACD, OBV, 200-day EMA.
Backtesting Rolling test dates, point-in-time filtering, excess returns, rank correlation.
Text Signals RSS and XML parsing, regex keyword scoring, date extraction, proximity rules.
Automation Windows Task Scheduler, run logs, Telegram Bot API, config-based credentials.
Validation A forward-test logger that checks every alert 3, 7 and 14 days later.

A backtest that proves you wrong is worth more than a strategy that looks good.

The scanner is now forward-testing live. Whatever the results say, the process is the part I'm proudest of: build it, test it honestly, and let the data decide what stays.
