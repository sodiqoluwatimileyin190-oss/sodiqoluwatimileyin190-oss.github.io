The Business Problem

How do you build a realistic retail dataset when the data doesn't already exist?

Retail analytics depends on data that reflects how a real business actually operates. A simple random dataset would not capture the relationships between customers, products, branches, payments, seasonality and purchasing behaviour.

The objective was therefore to design a retail data environment where those relationships were explicitly defined and then translated into interconnected synthetic data.

Project Disclaimer

JRAD Retail is a fictional and simulated retail environment created for portfolio and analytical purposes. The project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or based on proprietary data from any real retailer.

The business scenarios and operational behaviours were informed by real-world observations of Nigerian superstore environments and translated into custom business rules, probability distributions and simulation logic.

Building the Retail Foundation

Designing the inputs before generating the transactions.

The system began with structured reference libraries and business foundations that would later drive the Python generation process.

Artifact 01

Product Master

The Product Master served as the commercial foundation of the JRAD Retail ecosystem, defining the products available across the simulated business and establishing attributes that influence purchasing, pricing, demand, storage and downstream transaction behaviour.

Artifact 02

Distribution Library

A custom Distribution Library was created in Excel to define realistic probability distributions and business rules for customer and transaction behaviour.

Branch-level customer distribution, age, demographic patterns, registration behaviour, loyalty membership, payment methods, basket sizes and acquisition channels were incorporated into the modelling framework.
Artifact 03

Nigerian Identity Library

A dedicated Identity Library was created to generate realistic Nigerian customer identities using structured pools of first names, surnames, gender-specific names and ethnic name groups.

Artifact 04

Customer Master Output

The generation process combined identity data, demographic distributions, branch logic, registration behaviour, loyalty status, acquisition channels and other defined business rules to produce structured customer records.

Artifact 05

Business Events & Seasonality

Simulated business events were incorporated to influence customer traffic, basket size, spending, product demand and peak shopping hours.

Python Data Generation

Turning business rules into large-scale synthetic data.

A custom Python-based transaction generation engine was developed to simulate realistic retail activity across the JRAD Retail Data System. The generator applied business rules, customer behaviour, branch activity, product demand, pricing logic and transaction patterns to create the final large-scale dataset.

Generation Engine

Transaction Generation Engine

The Python engine transformed the defined retail rules and behavioural distributions into interconnected transaction activity.

Generation Output

Large-Scale Dataset Generation

The generation process produced 225,499 receipts and more than 1.8 million transaction lines, creating a large-scale simulated retail dataset for further analysis in SQL, Excel and Power BI.
