# JRAD Retail Power BI Dashboard

## Executive Intelligence & Visual Engineering

A Power BI retail analytics solution built from the JRAD Superstores 2026 retail data ecosystem.

This project transforms transactional retail data into executive-level business intelligence through Power BI, DAX, time intelligence, Deneb/Vega-Lite visual engineering, custom dashboard design and data storytelling.

---

## Chapter 1 — Executive, Sales & Customer Intelligence

### 01 — Executive Overview

The executive performance overview provides a consolidated view of revenue, target achievement, branch performance, department contribution, customer persona performance and major business insights.

### 02 — Sales & Time Intelligence

Explores revenue behaviour across salary cycles, time periods, business events, demand patterns and basket performance.

### 03 — Customer Segmentation

Examines customer personas, shopping behaviour, revenue contribution and the relationship between customer characteristics and purchasing patterns.
