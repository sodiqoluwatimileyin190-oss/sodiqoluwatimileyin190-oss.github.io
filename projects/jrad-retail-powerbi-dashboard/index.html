The Business Question

What is actually driving retail performance?

The dashboard was designed to answer four management-level questions:

How is the business performing?
When does demand peak?
Which branches and departments drive revenue?
Which customer personas create the most value?

Rather than presenting isolated charts, the solution connects these questions into a single analytical story across executive performance, time intelligence and customer segmentation.

Dashboard

Three analytical perspectives.

Each page answers a different business question while maintaining a consistent executive visual language.

Page 01

Executive Overview

A single-glance view of company-wide performance, revenue movement, branch execution, department contribution and customer value.

Key Finding

December delivered ₦1.90bn in revenue — the strongest month of the year — closing 2026 with an explosive festive-season finish.

Strategic Focus

Protect festive-season momentum, investigate the June slowdown, and tighten branch-level target setting to close the gap between company-wide growth and individual branch performance.

Page 02

Sales & Time Intelligence

Understanding when and how demand happens across the day, week, salary cycle and major business events.

Key Finding

Demand peaks around 6 PM, concentrated in the 5–7 PM evening rush, while Saturday records the highest demand of the week.

Strategic Focus

Prioritize peak-hour staffing, event-driven inventory planning, and salary-cycle promotions to meet demand where it is concentrated.

Page 03

Customer Segmentation

Understanding who shops, how they spend, and what drives customer value across personas and shopping missions.

Key Finding

Premium and Family Shopper personas combine high basket value with strong purchase frequency, making them the standout high-value segments.

Strategic Focus

Prioritize Premium and Family Shoppers, convert high-frequency walk-ins into registered customers, and reinforce Weekly Grocery as the core revenue driver.

Key Findings

The numbers tell a clear business story.

The dashboard surfaces patterns that management can translate directly into operational decisions.

01

Festive momentum

December delivered ₦1.90bn, making it the strongest revenue month of 2026 and highlighting the importance of festive-season demand.

02

June slowdown

June recorded the sharpest monthly decline, falling 12.7% before revenue recovered in subsequent months.

03

Uneven branch execution

Most individual branches fell short of target even while JRAD maintained strong overall growth, revealing a gap between company-level performance and branch execution.

04

Demand concentration

Customer activity concentrates around the evening rush, with 6 PM emerging as the peak demand hour and Saturday as the strongest day.

05

Event-driven revenue

Major business events generated a combined ₦2.32bn uplift above baseline, representing a 17.8% increase.

06

High-value customers

Premium and Family Shoppers combine strong basket value and purchase frequency, making them important targets for retention and personalized offers.

Data Model Architecture

The analytical architecture behind the dashboard.

The report is supported by a structured Power BI data model connecting transactional facts with customer, product, branch, date, pricing, behavioral and event dimensions.

Full Power BI Model View showing the relationships between the project's fact tables, dimension tables, behavioral tables, business-event tables and centralized measures layer. The model was designed to support consistent filtering, time intelligence and reusable analytical calculations.

Technical Implementation

A structured analytical foundation.

The dashboard was built around a star-schema approach with dedicated DAX measures and custom visual components.

Data Modeling

A structured star schema connects FactSales, FactInventory and FactPayments with dimensions including DimDate, DimProduct, DimCustomer and branch and business-event tables.

DAX & Time Intelligence

DAX measures power growth calculations, target achievement, variance analysis, time-based comparisons, gauges and dynamic business insights.

Conditional Formatting

KPI indicator colors are driven by DAX measures, allowing visual states to respond dynamically to the underlying analytical results.

Deneb & Vega-Lite

Custom Deneb visuals extend native Power BI capabilities with bespoke KPI cards, gauges, flow visualizations and insight callouts.

Interactive UX

Synchronized Month, Branch and Department slicers, together with in-report navigation and cross-filtering, create a cohesive analytical experience.

Executive Storytelling

Visual hierarchy, KPI prioritization, contextual insight panels and consistent visual language were used to move the report beyond descriptive reporting toward decision support.

Business Impact

Strengthen customer retention
