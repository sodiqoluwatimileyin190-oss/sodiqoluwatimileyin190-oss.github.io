01 / EXECUTIVE PERFORMANCE

Executive Performance Overview

A single-glance leadership view answering the central question: how is the business performing?

  • December delivered ₦1.90bn in revenue — the strongest month of the year.
  • June recorded the sharpest monthly decline, falling 12.7% before recovery.
  • Company-wide growth remained strong despite uneven branch-level target achievement.
Strategic Focus

Protect festive-season momentum, investigate the June slowdown, and tighten branch-level target setting to close the gap between company-wide growth and individual branch performance.

02 / SALES & TIME INTELLIGENCE

Sales & Time Intelligence

A demand-timing layer explaining when customers spend, how basket value changes through the salary cycle, and how events influence revenue.

  • Spending is strongest during Salary Week, with average basket value around ₦79K.
  • Demand peaks around 6 PM, concentrated within the 5–7 PM evening rush, with Saturday as the highest-demand day.
  • Business events generated a combined ₦2.32bn uplift, representing approximately 17.8% above baseline.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize peak-hour staffing, event-driven inventory planning, and salary-cycle promotions to meet demand where it is concentrated.

03 / CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Customer Segmentation

A customer-value layer explaining who shops, how they spend, how frequently they purchase, and where value concentrates.

  • Premium and Family Shopper personas combine high basket value with strong purchase frequency, making them standout high-value segments.
  • Weekly Grocery accounts for roughly 85% of shopping-mission revenue, making it the core driver of overall spend.
  • Walk-in customers dominate the customer mix, creating an opportunity to convert frequent walk-ins into registered customers.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize Premium and Family Shopper segments, convert high-frequency walk-ins into registered customers, and reinforce Weekly Grocery as the core revenue driver.
