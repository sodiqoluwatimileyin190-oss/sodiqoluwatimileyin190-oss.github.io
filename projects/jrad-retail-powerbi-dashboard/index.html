The Business Question

What is driving retail performance?

The dashboard was designed to answer four management-level questions: how the business is performing, when demand is concentrated, which branches and departments are strongest, and which customer personas create the most value.

Decision Lens Revenue · Demand · Branches · Departments · Customers
Dashboard Intelligence

Three pages. One connected retail story.

Each page answers a different layer of the business question, moving from executive performance to demand behavior and customer value.

01 / EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Executive Performance Overview

A single-glance leadership view of revenue, baskets, basket value, target achievement, branch performance, departments and customer personas.

  • December delivered ₦1.90bn in revenue, the strongest month of the year.
  • June recorded the sharpest monthly decline, falling 12.7% before recovery.
  • Most individual branches fell short of target, while JRAD still maintained strong overall growth.
Strategic Focus

Protect festive-season momentum, investigate the June slowdown, and tighten branch-level target setting.

02 / SALES & TIME

Sales & Time Intelligence

A demand-behavior layer showing when and how customers spend across salary cycles, hours, weekdays, business events and shopping missions.

  • Spending is strongest during Salary Week, with average basket value around ₦79K.
  • Demand peaks around 6 PM, concentrated in the 5–7 PM evening rush, with Saturday as the highest-demand day.
  • Business events generated ₦2.32bn in combined uplift, or +17.8% above baseline.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize peak-hour staffing, event-driven inventory planning, and salary-cycle promotions.

03 / CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Customer Segmentation

A customer-value layer explaining who shops, how they spend, how frequently they purchase, and where value concentrates.

  • Premium and Family Shopper personas combine high basket value with strong purchase frequency.
  • Weekly Grocery accounts for roughly 85% of shopping-mission revenue.
  • Walk-in customers dominate the mix, creating an opportunity to convert frequent walk-ins into registered customers.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize Premium and Family Shoppers, convert high-frequency walk-ins, and reinforce Weekly Grocery.

Technical Implementation

The analytical architecture behind the report.

Power BI & Data Modeling

End-to-end report development using a star-schema architecture connecting sales, inventory and supporting fact tables to business dimensions and a dedicated measures layer.

DAX & Time Intelligence

Dynamic measures support growth rates, target calculations, gauge logic, KPI indicators and natural-language insight text that responds to the selected context.

Conditional KPI Logic

KPI indicator colors are bound to DAX measure expressions, allowing visual states to update automatically with the underlying business results.

Deneb & Vega-Lite

Custom KPI cards, gauges, flow visuals and insight callouts extend the native Power BI visual language where more controlled analytical presentation was required.

Interactive UX

Consistent visual theming, synchronized Month, Branch and Department slicers, and in-report page navigation create a connected analytical experience across all three pages.

Executive Data Storytelling

The report is structured around business questions rather than simply displaying metrics, guiding leadership from performance to demand behavior and customer value.

Business Impact

What management can do with the findings.

Protect High-Value Customers

Improve Branch Execution

Use branch-level target performance to investigate execution gaps and make target setting more closely reflect local performance realities.
