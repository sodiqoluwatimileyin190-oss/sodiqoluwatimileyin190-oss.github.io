The Business Question

What is driving retail performance?

The dashboard was designed to answer four management-level questions: how the business is performing, when demand is concentrated, which branches and departments are strongest, and which customer personas create the most value.

Decision lens Revenue · Demand · Branches · Departments · Customers
Dashboard Intelligence

Three pages. One connected retail story.

Each page answers a different layer of the business question, moving from executive performance to time-based demand and finally to customer value.

01 / EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Executive Performance Overview

A single-glance leadership view of revenue, baskets, basket value, target achievement, branch performance, departments and customer personas.

  • December delivered ₦1.90bn in revenue, the strongest month of the year.
  • June recorded the sharpest monthly decline, falling 12.7% before recovery.
  • Most individual branches fell short of target, while the company still maintained strong overall growth.
Strategic Focus

Protect festive-season momentum, investigate the June slowdown, and tighten branch-level target setting.

02 / SALES & TIME

Sales & Time Intelligence

A demand-behavior layer showing when and how customers spend across salary cycles, hours, weekdays, business events and shopping missions.

  • Salary Week produced the strongest spending, with average basket value around ₦79K.
  • Demand peaks around 6 PM, concentrated in the 5–7 PM evening rush, with Saturday as the highest-demand day.
  • Business events generated ₦2.32bn in combined uplift, or +17.8% above baseline.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize peak-hour staffing, event-driven inventory planning, and salary-cycle promotions.

03 / CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Customer Segmentation

A customer-value layer explaining who shops, how they spend, how frequently they purchase, and where value concentrates.

  • Premium and Family Shopper personas combine high basket value with strong purchase frequency, making them standout high-value segments.
  • Weekly Grocery accounts for roughly 85% of shopping-mission revenue, making it the core driver of overall spend.
  • Walk-in customers dominate the customer mix, highlighting a clear opportunity to convert frequent walk-ins into registered customers.
Strategic Focus

Prioritize Premium and Family Shopper segments, convert high-frequency walk-ins into registered customers, and reinforce Weekly Grocery as the core revenue driver.

Data Model

The analytical architecture behind the dashboard.

The report is supported by a structured Power BI model combining transactional facts, behavioral dimensions, product and customer context, pricing history, business events, and a dedicated DAX measures layer.

Power BI Model View
Star-schema architecture connecting FactSales, FactInventory, FactPayments and supporting dimension tables including DimDate, DimProduct, DimCustomer, DimBranchBehaviour, DimMonthlyBehaviour, DimBusinessEvent and DimPricingHistory.

Technical Implementation

Built for analytical depth, not just visual appeal.

Dynamic growth-rate calculations, target and gauge logic, period comparisons, and dynamically generated natural-language insight measures.

Conditional KPI Logic
