The Business Question

What does the underlying retail data actually reveal?

Before building executive dashboards, the dataset needed to be interrogated at the source.

The SQL analysis was designed to answer questions around sales performance, revenue movement, customer behaviour, payment patterns, product performance, branch execution and inventory risk.

This created a validated analytical foundation for the subsequent Power BI reporting layer.
