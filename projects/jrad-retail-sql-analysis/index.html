The Business Question

What does the underlying retail data actually reveal?

Before building executive dashboards, the dataset needed to be interrogated at the source.

The SQL analysis was designed to answer questions around sales performance, revenue movement, customer behaviour, payment patterns, product performance, branch execution and inventory risk.

This created a validated analytical foundation for the subsequent Power BI reporting layer.

Analytical Scope

From transactional records to business questions.

SQL was used to move beyond simple querying and establish a structured understanding of how the retail business behaves.

01

Sales & Revenue

Examining revenue movement, transaction behaviour, sales patterns and the underlying performance of the retail operation.

02

Customer & Payment Behaviour

Analysing customer activity, basket behaviour and payment-method patterns to understand how customers interact with the business.

03

Product & Branch Performance

Comparing product-level and branch-level performance to identify important contributors and areas of operational variation.

04

Inventory & Stock Risk

Using inventory information to identify stock exposure, product availability issues and potential operational risks.
