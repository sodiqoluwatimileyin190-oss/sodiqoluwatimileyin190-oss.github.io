# Olist — Customer Retention & Operational Analytics

A focused Power BI and SQL analysis of the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset, examining customer retention, product performance, and operational delivery efficiency.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior and operational performance across the Olist marketplace from 2016–2018.

The analysis focuses on identifying retention challenges, high-churn product categories, and operational factors affecting delivery performance.

## Key Areas

- Customer retention and repeat purchase behavior
- Customer churn analysis
- Product category performance
- Product adoption and churn risk
- Delivery performance by region
- Order fulfillment and operational efficiency

## Key Findings

- Approximately **98% of customers made only one purchase**, highlighting a significant customer retention challenge.
- Repeat purchasing was extremely low, indicating limited customer loyalty within the marketplace.
- Several product categories recorded **97%–100% churn**, creating opportunities for product and customer-retention strategies.
- Delivery performance varied considerably across regions, with some states experiencing significantly longer delivery times.
- The analysis highlights the relationship between product adoption, revenue contribution, and customer retention.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard is organized into three analytical areas:

### 01 — Customer Retention
Examines customer purchasing frequency, repeat purchase behavior, average spend, and overall churn.

### 02 — Product Performance
Identifies high-performing, under-performing, and high-churn product categories while examining product adoption and retention risk.

### 03 — Operational Insights
Analyzes order fulfillment, delivery performance, cancellation rates, regional delivery variation, and fulfillment efficiency.

## Dataset

**Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset**

The dataset contains approximately 100K orders covering the period **2016–2018**.

## Project Outcome

The analysis demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be combined to transform transactional e-commerce data into actionable insights around **customer retention, product performance, and operational efficiency**.
