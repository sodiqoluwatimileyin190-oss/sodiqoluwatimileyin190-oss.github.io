01 · ANALYTICAL CONTEXT

From Transactions
to Customer Behaviour

The analysis moves beyond order volume to understand what happens after the first purchase — examining repeat behaviour, category performance, and the operational experience surrounding fulfilment.

Business Problem

Olist contains a large volume of transactional e-commerce data, but transaction volume alone does not explain whether customers return, which categories contribute to stronger retention, or whether operational performance may influence the customer experience.

  • Are customers returning after their first order?
  • Which product categories show stronger or weaker retention?
  • Where are churn risks concentrated?
  • How does delivery performance vary across regions?

Analytical Approach

SQL was used to combine and aggregate transactional data into customer, product, and operational views. Power BI then transformed these analytical outputs into an interactive decision-support dashboard.

  • Customer-level purchase behaviour
  • Repeat purchase and churn classification
  • Product/category performance
  • Delivery and fulfilment analysis

03 · KEY FINDINGS

What the Data
Revealed

The analysis identified a significant retention challenge alongside meaningful differences in product and operational performance.

01

Extremely Low Repeat Purchasing

Approximately 98% of customers were classified as churned under the project's 90-day inactivity definition, highlighting a major customer retention challenge.

02

Category-Level Retention Differences

Product categories showed substantial differences in revenue contribution, customer adoption, and churn behaviour, creating opportunities for more targeted retention strategies.

03

Delivery Performance Varies by Region

Delivery and fulfilment performance varied considerably across regions, providing an operational dimension to the customer experience analysis.
